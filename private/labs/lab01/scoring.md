# Lab 01 — Scoring Sheet (standard two-band pattern)

**Weight: 0% (setup lab).** Scored anyway so students meet the system and we calibrate it.

## Band A · Automated — 80 pts (from check-ta.sh over submitted bundles)

| Item | pts |
|---|---|
| TEAM.txt names the team | 8 |
| passport.txt records the machine | 8 |
| hello.c edited (placeholder gone) | 10 |
| hello compiles and runs | 12 |
| output.txt matches ./hello | 12 |
| notes/README.txt | 8 |
| session.txt recorded | 12 |
| kill-evidence in session | 10 |
| *(bonus)* replay.sh runs cleanly | +5 |

## Band B · Discretionary — 20 pts

One judgment per team — engagement, independence, workspace discipline,
assessed as usual by asking a member to walk through their session log.
**Full (20) is the default**; Partial (10) or Minimal (0) require a one-line note.
