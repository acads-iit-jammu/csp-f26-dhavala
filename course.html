Weekly Plan (Lectures)

The plan below is tentative and will be adjusted as the semester progresses. Slides marked Lec n are from CS1100, IIT Madras, reused with permission — see acknowledgements. Additional notes will be posted as we go.

Each week’s page carries Pre-read, In-Class, and After-Class sections — and ends with PAGAL Notes (Perspectives on Algorithms, Generative AI & Learning): an informal, blog-style weekly column on how that week’s material looks in the age of AI.

Week Dates (Mon, Tue) Topics Materials
01 Aug 3, Aug 4 Introduction: computer science, algorithms, computer hardware & software Lec 1 · Lec 3
02 Aug 10, Aug 11 Mon: the computer revisited — von Neumann architecture, the Toy Computer (stored programs vs calculators). Tue: number systems — radix-q, binary & hex, conversions; signed integers; fixed & floating point, IEEE FP32 Deck 04 · Toy Computer · Under the Hood 1–3 · Deck 03 · Lec 3
03 Aug 17, Aug 18 Number systems (contd.): binary arithmetic, floating point; conversions. Class Test-1 window opens Aug 19 Notes (TBP)
04 Aug 24, Aug 25 Programming types (declarative, imperative); structure of a C program; our first C program Lec 4
05 Aug 31, Sep 1 Compilation pipeline: source, assembly and object files, linker, loader; compiler vs. interpreter Lec 6
06 Sep 7, Sep 8 Data & expressions: literals, variables, identifiers, data types; operators, precedence; printf/scanf Lec 5
07 Sep 14, Sep 15 Control structures: boolean expressions, selection (if, if-else, switch) Lec 7 · Lec 8
08 Sep 21, Sep 22 Mid-Semester Examinations (Sep 18–23) — no classes —
09 Sep 28, Sep 29 Mon: Iteration: while, do-while, for. Tue: Problem-solving session — loop workouts: mini calculator, primes, GCD, min Lec 9 · Lec 12 · Lec 13 · Lec 14 · Lec 16
10 Oct 5, Oct 6 Arrays: one-dimensional and character arrays; examples Lec 15
11 Oct 12, Oct 13 Strings and string processing; multi-dimensional arrays; matrix operations. Class Test-2 window opens Oct 14 Lec 17 · Lec 18
12 Oct 19, Oct 20 Dussehra holidays — no classes —
13 Oct 26, Oct 27 Functions and modular programming; scope; recursion Lec 19 · Lec 20 · Lec 24
14 Nov 2, Nov 3 Pointers; dynamic memory allocation (malloc, calloc, realloc, free) Lec 27 (pointers part) · Lec 28 · OCW L11
15 Nov 9, Nov 10 Holidays (Govardhan Puja, Declared Holiday) — no classes —
16 Nov 16, Nov 17 Function pointers; structures & unions Lec 29 · Lec 30 · OCW L6 · OCW L8
MU-1 TBA Make-up class: file handling and error handling (fopen/fclose, formatted file I/O, errno, perror); header files and modular builds OCW L4 · OCW L10 · OCW L3
MU-2 TBA Make-up class: applications; brief of object-oriented programming; course wrap-up Wrap-up · Notes (TBP)

The syllabus allocates 28 lecture hours, but the holiday weeks above leave only 26 Mon/Tue slots — so the two make-up classes (MU-1, MU-2) are required, not optional. They will be scheduled on designated compensatory days and announced in class and here. Materials marked OCW are from MIT OCW 6.087 (CC BY-NC-SA).
